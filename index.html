📚 Nutrition Guide
⚡ Energy Balance▶

Weight management comes down to energy balance: calories consumed vs calories burned.

  • 🔻 Calorie deficit = weight loss
  • ➡️ Calorie balance = maintenance
  • 🔺 Calorie surplus = weight gain

TDEE breakdown: BMR (60-70%) + TEF (~10%) + NEAT (15-20%) + Exercise (5-10%)

💡 NEAT often drops when dieting. Keep your step count at 7,000–10,000/day.
🥩 Protein — The Priority Macro▶

Protein is the most important macro for body composition. It builds muscle, keeps you full, and burns the most calories during digestion (20-30% TEF).

GoalTarget
General Health1.5 g/kg
Fat Loss / Muscle1.8–2.2 g/kg

Best sources: Chicken breast (31g/100g), salmon (25g/100g), eggs (6g each), Greek yoghurt (10g/100g), tofu (17g/100g).

Spread protein across 3-5 meals, aiming for 25-40g per meal.

🍚 Carbohydrates▶

Carbs are your body's preferred fuel, especially for exercise and brain function. After protein and fat are set, remaining calories come from carbs.

Best sources: Oats, rice, potatoes, whole grain bread, quinoa, fruits, beans.

80/20 rule: ~80% whole foods, ~20% whatever you enjoy. No food is off-limits!
🥑 Fats — Essential for Hormones▶

Dietary fat is essential for hormones, brain function, and vitamin absorption. Minimum: 0.7g per kg body weight.

Best sources: Avocados, nuts, olive oil, fatty fish, seeds, eggs, dark chocolate (70%+).

⚠️ Fat is 9 cal/g vs 4 cal/g for protein/carbs. Measure oils and nut butters!
🥦 Fiber & Micronutrients▶

Fiber target: 14g per 1,000 calories. Keeps you full, supports gut health.

Key micros for women: Iron (energy), Calcium (bones), Vitamin D (immune), Folate (cell growth), Omega-3 (brain), Magnesium (sleep).

💡 Eat a variety of colourful fruits and veg daily — "eat the rainbow" 🌈
📊 Diet Phases▶

Fat Loss (Cut): 250-500 cal deficit, 8-16 weeks max, keep protein high. Rate: 0.5-1% body weight/week.

Maintenance: Eat at TDEE. Do this at least as long as your cut. Cements habits.

Lean Gain: 200-300 cal surplus, ~0.25 kg/week for women. Pair with resistance training.

Reverse Diet: After a cut, add 50-100 cal/week back to maintenance over 4-8 weeks to avoid rebound.

💊 Supplements▶

✅ Worth it: Creatine (3-5g/day), Vitamin D (1-2000 IU), Omega-3 (if not eating fish 2×/week), protein powder.

⚠️ Optional: Caffeine pre-workout, magnesium for sleep, iron if blood tests show deficiency.

❌ Skip: Fat burners, detox teas, BCAAs (if eating enough protein).

🧠 Mindset & Habits▶
  • Progress, not perfection — within 5-10g is close enough
  • Consistency beats intensity — 80% for 12 months > 100% for 3 weeks
  • No food is "bad" — it's the overall pattern
  • Weight fluctuates daily — track weekly averages
  • One bad day doesn't undo weeks of progress
💡 The best diet is the one you can stick to. If it makes you miserable, it's not the right plan.
